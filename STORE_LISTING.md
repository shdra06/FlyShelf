## 1. Product Name
FlyShelf

## 2. Description
FlyShelf is the ultimate premium cross-device clipboard manager and productivity ecosystem designed for power users, developers, and creators. It seamlessly captures and organizes everything you copy—text, images, files, URLs, and code snippets—into an elegant, searchable, floating glassmorphic dashboard.

Whether you're juggling code, organizing research, merging PDFs, or needing instant AI-powered OCR, FlyShelf keeps your workflow uninterrupted. 

**Key Features:**
• **Universal Clipboard History:** Automatically saves everything you copy. Find past clips instantly with lightning-fast search and categorization.
• **Drag-and-Drop Workspace:** Drag files in to add them to your clipboard stack, or drag items out into other apps like Outlook or Word.
• **AI NPU-Accelerated OCR:** Extract text from any image instantly using the cutting-edge Windows AI TextRecognizer engine (optimized for Windows 11 24H2+ NPUs).
• **Natural Language Reminders:** Type naturally like "remind me in 30 minutes to check the build" and FlyShelf handles the rest.
• **PDF Toolkit:** Merge, rearrange, and extract pages from multiple PDFs right from your clipboard. Convert clipboard images directly to PDF.
• **Smart Context Actions:** Auto-solves math equations, cleans UTM tracking links, extracts tables from screenshots, and scans QR codes instantly.
• **Mica Glass Design:** A breathtaking Windows 11 native interface featuring 7 premium color themes and custom wallpaper support.

**Important Note for Microsoft Store Users:**
To comply with Microsoft Store policies, the "Global Transfer" feature is disabled in this Store version. You can still enjoy the full suite of local productivity features, and Pro features can be unlocked easily via the Microsoft Store Pro add-on.
