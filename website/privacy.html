🔒 Privacy Policy

Your Privacy, Protected

FlyShelf is built privacy-first. Your clipboard data never leaves your devices without your explicit action.

📅 Last Updated: May 27, 2026 👤 Developer: Shivendra 📧 shdra06@gmail.com

TL;DR — The Short Version

  • Your clipboard data is stored only on your own devices — never on our servers
  • Zero telemetry — we do not track usage, analytics, or behavior
  • All data in transit is encrypted with AES-256-GCM end-to-end
  • Data at rest is protected by Windows DPAPI per-user encryption
  • We only collect what is needed to process your Pro license purchase
  • You can delete everything instantly from within the app

1 What We Store on Your Device

FlyShelf operates primarily as a local application. All data is stored on your physical machine under your user account.

Clipboard Content

  • Text and Code: Stored in %AppData%\FlyShelf\clipboard_history.json
  • Images and Files: Cached in %AppData%\FlyShelf\Images\
  • Retention: Configurable — 1, 7, 14, or 30 days. Capped at 500 items by default

Password Protection

Items detected as passwords are encrypted at rest using Windows DPAPI anchored to your Windows user account. No other user or machine can decrypt them.

App Credentials

  • Firebase Auth Token: Encrypted in %AppData%\FlyShelf\firebase_auth.dat
  • Gemini API Key (optional): Encrypted in %AppData%\FlyShelf\config.json
  • Pro License: DPAPI-encrypted in %AppData%\FlyShelf\license.json

2 Network and Transit Security

PC Companion ---AES-256-GCM---> LAN Sync (direct) PC Companion ---AES-256-GCM---> Cloudflare Tunnel ---AES-256-GCM---> Android PC Companion --- Encrypted signals only ---> Firebase (NO clipboard data)

Local LAN Sync

Direct TCP transfer encrypted with AES-256-GCM using keys derived via PBKDF2-SHA256 (100,000 iterations).

Remote Sync via Cloudflare

Ephemeral tunnels, created on demand and destroyed on app close. Payloads are AES-256-GCM encrypted at the app level — Cloudflare cannot read your clipboard data.

Firebase Signaling

No clipboard data is ever stored in Firebase. Firebase is used only as a secure phonebook to help devices find each other.

4 Pro License and Purchases

When you purchase FlyShelf Pro we collect only:

  • License key — generated at purchase, stored DPAPI-encrypted on your device
  • Device ID — a randomly generated anonymous identifier
  • Activation timestamp — for the 3-device limit

Payment details (card number, billing address) are handled entirely by Stripe or the Microsoft Store. We never see or store your payment information.

5 Your Rights and Data Control

  • Inspect: Browse %AppData%\FlyShelf\ directly to see every file the app creates
  • Partial delete: Delete individual clipboard entries or use time-range sweeps from the dashboard
  • Full wipe: Use "Uninstall FlyShelf" from System Logs — wipes all data and exits
  • Deactivate license: Remove your Pro license from Settings at any time
