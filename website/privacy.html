Privacy policy

Your clipboard stays yours.

What you copy can be personal. This page explains what FlyShelf keeps on your devices, what leaves them and why, and what we store when you buy Pro.

In short

  • Your clipboard history is stored on your own devices. It isn’t uploaded to FlyShelf servers.
  • FlyShelf contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.
  • Passwords and API keys you copy are detected and encrypted at rest with Windows DPAPI.
  • Sync goes directly between your devices on a local network, or through a temporary Cloudflare tunnel if you use remote sync.
  • Firebase only helps your devices find each other. Clipboard content is never stored there.
  • For Pro purchases we keep your email, license key and device activations. Razorpay handles payment details.

1What FlyShelf stores on your device

FlyShelf is a local application. Its data lives in your Windows user profile, under %AppData%\FlyShelf\.

Clipboard history

  • Text, links and code: clipboard_history.json, with recent changes in clipboard_journal.jsonl.
  • Images and files: cached in Images\.
  • How long it’s kept: 7 days by default. You can choose 14 or 30 days, or no expiry with Pro. History holds up to 50,000 items.

Sensitive items

Clips that look like passwords or API keys are encrypted at rest with Windows DPAPI, which ties them to your Windows user account. Other history is stored as ordinary files in your user profile, protected by your Windows account permissions rather than additional encryption.

Notes, shortcuts and settings

  • Notes, to-dos and reminders: notes.json, todos.json and reminders.json.
  • Text shortcuts: encrypted with DPAPI.
  • Firebase sign-in token: encrypted in firebase_auth.dat.
  • AI provider API keys (optional): encrypted in config.json.
  • Pro license: DPAPI-encrypted in license.json.

2Sync and network connections

Pairing

You pair devices by scanning a QR code or entering a six-character code that expires after five minutes. Pairing details and the device addresses kept for discovery are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, using keys derived with PBKDF2-SHA256 (100,000 iterations).

Local network sync

On the same network, your devices connect to each other directly. FlyShelf’s direct-transfer connections are authenticated with a key from pairing, but some transfers — including file transfers — are not separately encrypted by FlyShelf. Use local sync on networks you trust.

Remote sync through Cloudflare

If you use remote sync, FlyShelf starts a temporary Cloudflare Quick Tunnel so your phone can reach your PC from another network. The tunnel is created on demand and closes when FlyShelf exits. Traffic reaches Cloudflare over HTTPS and is relayed to your PC; Cloudflare handles it under its own privacy policy.

Firebase

Firebase is used only so your devices can find each other. It holds an anonymous sign-in ID, your active devices with their encrypted addresses, temporary pairing codes and device groups. No clipboard content is sent to or stored in Firebase.

Other connections the app makes

  • Update checks with GitHub, to find and verify new releases.
  • Time checks with Cloudflare (1.1.1.1), worldtimeapi.org and timeapi.io, plus lightweight requests to google.com, cloudflare.com and microsoft.com.
  • Resources for tools: a font list for PDF tools from GitHub’s API, and the Mermaid diagram library from jsDelivr for Markdown previews.
  • Cloudflare’s tunnel program (cloudflared), downloaded when remote sync first needs it.
  • AI features, only if you add your own API key: the content you choose is sent to the provider you picked — Google Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic. Regular OCR runs on your PC and doesn’t need this.

4Pro license and purchases

When you buy FlyShelf Pro, we keep:

  • Your email address — to send your license key, and to resend it if you lose it.
  • Your license key and order reference — to confirm the purchase.
  • Device ID — a random identifier generated by the app.
  • Activation time — to apply the three-device limit.
  • Subscription status (monthly and yearly plans) — the Razorpay subscription reference, plan, status and the date the paid period ends, so Pro stays on while your plan is active.

To prevent abuse, the purchase, activation, recovery and subscription-management endpoints also record a one-way hash of your IP address. These records are deleted after 24 hours.

Card, UPI and bank details are entered in Razorpay’s checkout and handled entirely by Razorpay. We never see or store them.

5This website

This site has no analytics or advertising cookies. It remembers your light or dark theme and your motion preference in your browser’s local storage. Fonts load from Google Fonts, and the pricing page loads Razorpay’s checkout script. The live demo on the homepage runs entirely in your browser; nothing you type into it is sent anywhere.

6Your control over your data

  • Inspect: open %AppData%\FlyShelf\ to see every file the app creates.
  • Delete: remove single clips, or clear a time range from the dashboard.
  • Uninstall: use “Uninstall FlyShelf” in About to remove the app and keep your data, or wipe everything.
  • License: remove your Pro license from Settings at any time.
