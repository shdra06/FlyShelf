Questions about Pro

Do monthly and yearly plans renew automatically?

Yes, until you cancel. Stop renewal any time under Manage subscription. You won’t be charged again, and Pro stays on until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

Can I still pay once?

Yes. Lifetime is a one-time payment of ₹1,999 or $50, with nothing to renew. Keys bought at the earlier lifetime price stay Pro for life.

What happens if a subscription ends?

Pro stays on until the paid period ends, plus three days in case a renewal is late. After that FlyShelf goes back to the Free plan.

Do I need an account?

No account is needed — just an email address, so we can send your key.

How many devices can I use?

One key activates Pro on up to three of your devices, on any plan.

What if I stay on Free?

That’s fine. Clipboard capture, OCR, image tools, video compression and conversions are unlimited on Free; a few heavier tools have daily limits.
