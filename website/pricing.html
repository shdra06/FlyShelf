Questions

How do I cancel?

Open FlyShelf Hub → Settings → License and press Stop renewal, or use Manage your plan above. You won’t be charged again, and Pro stays on until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

What happens when a subscription ends?

Pro stays on until the paid period ends, plus three days in case a renewal is late. Then FlyShelf goes back to the Free plan. Nothing you saved is lost.

How do I activate Pro?

Your key appears right after payment and is emailed to you. With FlyShelf running, press Activate on this PC, or paste the key in FlyShelf Hub → Settings → License.

How many devices can I use?

One key turns on Pro on up to three of your devices, on any plan. Turning off Pro on a PC frees its place.

Do I need an account?

No. Just an email address, so we can send your key.
