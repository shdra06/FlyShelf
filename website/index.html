How it works

Copy as usual. Find it later.

FlyShelf runs quietly in the background. Every copy lands on your shelf, so the thing you need is a shortcut and a few letters away.

  1. 1

    Copy anything

    Text, links, code, images and files are saved the moment you copy them. No extra clicks.

  2. 2

    Press Alt + C

    Your shelf opens over whatever you’re doing. You can change the shortcut — or shake your mouse while dragging.

  3. 3

    Search, pin, reuse

    Type a word to narrow it down, pin what you paste every day, then copy or drag it back out.

Privacy

Your clipboard stays yours.

What you copy is personal. FlyShelf keeps it on your devices and is clear about the few services it uses.

Read the privacy policy

  • Stored on your PC

    Your history lives in your Windows user folder, not on our servers.

  • No tracking

    No analytics, ads or crash-reporting services are built into the app.

  • Secrets encrypted

    Copied passwords and API keys are detected and encrypted with Windows DPAPI.

  • Discovery, not storage

    Firebase only helps your devices find each other. Your clips are never stored there.

Questions

Good to know before you start.

Something else on your mind? Email shdra06@gmail.com.

Is FlyShelf really free?

Yes. Clipboard capture, OCR, image tools, video compression and conversions to PDF, Markdown and spreadsheets are unlimited on Free, along with Android sync, notes and text shortcuts. A few heavier tools have daily limits — for example 100 PDF merges and 5 PDF compressions a day. Pro removes every limit, monthly, yearly or as a one-time purchase. The plan comparison lists every limit.

How do I open my shelf?

Press Alt + C from any app. You can pick a different shortcut in Settings. Shake-to-summon is on by default too: hold a mouse button and give the cursor a quick shake — handy in the middle of a drag.

How does sync with my Android phone work?

Install FlyShelf on your PC and phone, then pair them with a QR code or a six-character code. On the same network, clips and files travel directly between the two. Away from home, remote sync connects them through a temporary Cloudflare tunnel while your PC is on.

Where is my clipboard stored?

On your PC, in your Windows user folder. History is kept for 7 days by default; you can choose 14 or 30 days, or no expiry with Pro. Passwords and API keys you copy are encrypted with Windows DPAPI. The privacy policy lists every service the app talks to.

Does OCR need an internet connection?

No. FlyShelf uses the OCR built into Windows, which needs the matching language installed. On newer Copilot+ PCs it can use Windows AI text recognition. If you prefer a cloud model, you can add your own Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic API key.

Which versions of Windows and Android are supported?

Windows 10 version 2004 or later, and Windows 11. The Android app needs Android 7.0 or later.
