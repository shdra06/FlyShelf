Outdated Interfaces

Legacy managers rely on clunky, early-2000s layouts that disrupt focus and break modern desktop aesthetics.

Isolated Systems

Feature Deep-Dive

Engineered for Power Users

Every corner is engineered to eliminate friction, optimize speed, and enhance your digital clipboard flow.

Mica Sumner Overlay

A Windows desktop summoning hub using beautiful Windows 11 Mica blur and Acrylic backdrops. Out of sight, summonable instantly via Alt + c.

Kotlin Foreground Service

The Android companion uses a secure, energy-efficient native foreground service ensuring background clip listeners stay alive even under strict screen locks.

Spacebar QuickLook

Preview any document, image, or zip archive in seconds. Simply select any card on the shelf and tap Spacebar, exactly like macOS QuickLook.

Auto-Classification Badges

Smart regex engines analyze text and files, marking them with tags (JSON, C++, PYTHON, LINK, TERM) for structured context-sorting.

Atomic Pin History

Items marked as pinned survive bulk logs cleanup. Saves are fully debounced to avoid disk thrashing and written atomically via temporary buffers.

Taskbar Mini Widget

Keep a minimal, glassmorphic card pinned directly to your Windows Taskbar for real-time tracking of active inputs.

Privacy First. No Clouds Retained.

Unlike modern SaaS tools, FlyShelf keeps your data yours. Large files and credentials transfer entirely peer-to-peer over your local networks. Cloud storage channels are reserved solely for signaling relays and are set with 5-minute auto-purge timeouts. No telemetry tracking, no ads, no databases.
