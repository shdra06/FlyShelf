[x] PC CLIPBOARD SEARCH PERFORMANCE OPTIMIZATION

[x] MIXED FORMAT PDF/IMAGE MERGER & WORD CONVERSIONS

    - Protected mobile multi-select with a Microsoft Word Advisory block warning, preventing incompatible .docx native conversions on mobile and prompting paired PC companion instructions.
